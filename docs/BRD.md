# Business Requirements Document (BRD) - Startup OS & Customer Health Expansion Desk

## 1. Executive Summary
The Startup OS & Customer Health Expansion Desk is a comprehensive platform designed to empower startups and enterprises with real-time insights, customer health monitoring, and expansion opportunities. It leverages advanced AI agents to provide actionable intelligence, automate workflows, and drive growth.

## 2. Project Objectives
- To provide a centralized "Mission Control" interface for monitoring startup health.
- To use AI for real-time risk assessment and opportunity identification.
- To automate routine customer success and expansion tasks.
- To offer deep insights into customer usage, sentiment, and financial metrics.

## 3. Scope
- **User Management**: Role-Based Access Control (RBAC) for various stakeholders (Admins, CSMs, Sales, etc.).
- **Real-Time Dashboards**: Interactive visualizations of key performance indicators (KPIs).
- **AI Integration**: "NeuroLink" and "Agent Console" for AI-driven analysis and chat.
- **Modules**:
    - Vital Signs Dashboard
    - Usage Analytics
    - Customer 360
    - Growth Engine
    - Risk & Support
    - Integration Hub

## 4. Functional Requirements
- **Real-time Data Processing**: Ability to ingest and process data streams instantly.
- **Predictive Analytics**: Forecasting churn and beneficial expansion paths.
- **Workflow Automation**: Automated alerts and task generation based on health scores.
- **Collaboration Tools**: Integral chat and notification systems.

## 5. Non-Functional Requirements
- **Scalability**: Support for high volumes of data and concurrent users.
- **Security**: Enterprise-grade encryption and access controls.
- **Performance**: Sub-second latency for critical dashboard updates.
- **Usability**: intuitive, "Mission Control" aesthetic with dark mode and high contrast.

## 6. Constraints
- Must integrate with existing CRM and Support ticketing systems.
- Compliance with GDPR and other data privacy regulations.
